In short

  • The ramvo.app website uses no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We only store your chosen language and the visit source (for the life of the browser tab).
  • In the app, analytics runs only with your consent.

§1. What these technologies are

Cookies and similar technologies (localStorage, sessionStorage, app storage) are small pieces of data stored on your device. Storing information that is not strictly necessary for the service needs your consent (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, reg 6; Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law).

You can delete website data in your browser settings (clear site data). You can turn off analytics in the app settings. Data processing details: Privacy Policy.
