In short

  • Ramvo AI is software built on Anthropic's Claude language models. It is not a person or a doctor.
  • We always show when you are talking to AI and when you see content created by AI.
  • AI can be wrong. An AI plan is a suggestion you can change or reject.
  • We send the model provider only what is needed to answer, never your e-mail or name.

§1. What Ramvo AI is

  1. Ramvo AI is a set of features that use the Claude large language models provided by Anthropic, PBC (US), through their commercial API. Meal and ingredient illustrations are created by an image generation model provided by Pollinations.ai.
  2. Ramvo AI is an "AI system intended to interact with natural persons" under Article 50 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act). It is not a high-risk system: it is not used to diagnose, treat or decide on access to services.

§3. Labelling

  1. The Ramvo AI chat is labelled as a conversation with AI from the first message.
  2. Plans, recipes and illustrations created by AI are labelled "AI-generated" until a person (you or a Trainer) changes them.
  3. A Trainer's profile bio written with AI help is the Trainer's own content, which they approve and are responsible for.

§4. Limits and safety

  1. A language model may give information that is wrong, out of date or unsuitable for you. It knows nothing about your health beyond what you enter.
  2. Ramvo AI does not replace a doctor, dietitian or physiotherapist. If you have symptoms, pain, are pregnant, have a chronic condition or an eating disorder, talk to a professional.
  3. Ramvo AI has usage limits (currently up to 10 chat messages a day). Plans generated for a Trainer's clients can be reviewed and changed by the Trainer.
  4. If an AI answer seems harmful, report it to {{EMAIL}}. We will look into it and improve our safeguards.

§5. What data goes to the model provider

  1. We send Anthropic your question and the data needed to answer it: for example goal, level, age, sex, weight, equipment, food preferences, health limitations (if you consented to them) and part of your current plan. We do not send your e-mail, name, phone number or photos.
  2. We send Pollinations.ai only a description of the dish or ingredient, with no personal data.
  3. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through the API is not used to train models and is kept only for a limited time for safety purposes. Transfer details: Privacy Policy, §6.
  4. We do not use your data to train AI models of our own.
