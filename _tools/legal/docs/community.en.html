In short

  • Respect others. Don't post content that is illegal, abusive or dangerous to health.
  • Report content or a person in the app or at info@ramvo.app. Reports about a risk to life or content involving minors come first.
  • We explain every decision. You can appeal within 6 months.

§1. What is not allowed

  1. Illegal content, including child sexual abuse material, threats, incitement to violence or hatred, copyright infringement, fraud.
  2. Harassment: insults, intimidation, unwanted sexual content, persistently messaging someone against their wishes, sharing other people's personal data.
  3. Content dangerous to health: encouraging self-harm, extreme fasting or eating disorders, the use of anabolic steroids or other prohibited substances, dangerous "challenges".
  4. Selling prohibited substances, prescription medicines or products that need a licence.
  5. Deception: fake profiles, impersonation, fake qualifications, fake reviews, spam.
  6. Sexual content and nudity, except body progress photos shared privately with your own Trainer.
  7. Abusing the Service: getting around limits and security, automated data collection, malware.

§2. How to report content or a person

  1. In the app, use "Report" on a Trainer's profile, a review or a message. You can also e-mail {{EMAIL}}. Anyone can report, including people without an account (Article 16 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, the DSA).
  2. Tell us what you are reporting (a link, screenshot or description of where it is), why you think it is illegal or against the rules, and your e-mail, unless you are reporting child sexual abuse material; such a report may be anonymous.
  3. We confirm receipt. Reports about a risk to life, a child's safety or violence are handled without delay, usually within 24 hours, and others within 7 days.
  4. Where content may show a crime threatening someone's life or safety, we tell the relevant authorities. We report child sexual abuse material to the National Crime Agency or the Internet Watch Foundation (UK) or Dyżurnet.pl (PL).
  5. If you are in danger, call 999 (UK) or 112 (EU). Crisis support: in the UK Samaritans on 116 123; in Poland 116 123 (adults) and 116 111 (children and young people).

§3. How we decide

  1. A person reviews every report. We assess content against the law and these rules, taking into account context, severity and repetition.
  2. Possible decisions: no action, hiding or removing content, a warning, restricting features (for example chat or profile visibility), suspending or deleting the account.
  3. We send the person affected a statement of reasons: what we did, why, on which facts, whether we found the content illegal or against the rules, and how to appeal (Article 17 DSA). We tell the person who reported the outcome.
  4. We do not remove content automatically without a person involved.

§4. Appeals

  1. You can appeal a decision within 6 months by replying to the decision e-mail or writing to {{EMAIL}}. Appeals are free.
  2. An appeal is reviewed by a different person from the one who made the decision, usually within 14 days. If we agree with you, we restore the content or account.
  3. You can also go to court. People in the EU can use a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body (Article 21 DSA).

§5. Misuse of reporting

If someone frequently sends reports that are obviously unfounded, after a warning we may stop handling their reports for a period (Article 23 DSA).
