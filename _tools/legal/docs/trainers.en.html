In short

  • This is an agreement between you as a business and Ramvo. It applies together with the Terms of Service.
  • You pay for a plan by number of active clients. Your clients never pay Ramvo.
  • If your plan lapses, the panel becomes read-only and your clients keep training.
  • Your videos, plans and brand stay yours. You only give us a licence to show them to your clients.
  • For your clients' data you are a separate data controller. You protect it and use it only to coach the client.

§2. Trainer account and review

  1. A trainer account may be created by an adult who provides, or intends to provide, coaching services lawfully under the law of their country (for example a registered business, paying their taxes).
  2. You give true information about yourself, your qualifications, experience, prices and location. You do not claim certificates you do not hold.
  3. Before your profile appears in search we check that it is complete, credible and follows our rules, and we require a profile photo. We may ask for a document confirming your qualifications or business. We may refuse to publish a profile, giving the reason.
  4. We recommend professional indemnity and public liability insurance. Ramvo does not insure your services.
  5. You may invite clients from outside the app to Ramvo. You create an account for a client only with their agreement.

§3. Plans, limits and read-only mode

  1. Plans differ by the limit of active clients, meaning clients with an ongoing coaching relationship with you in Ramvo. Current plans: ramvo.app/pricing. Prices, the 30-day trial, renewal and cancelling are in the Subscriptions terms.
  2. When you reach the limit, your current clients stay, but you cannot accept new ones (requests from search, new client accounts) until you move to a higher plan or end the relationship with one of your clients.
  3. If your plan lapses, or a payment fails and the 7-day grace period ends, the panel runs in read-only mode: you see your data, chat with clients and handle existing bookings, but you cannot create or change plans, accept new clients or use AI features. Your clients still see the plans assigned to them and log their workouts.
  4. The Founding Trainer programme is described in §5 of the Subscriptions terms.

§4. Your content, videos and brand

  1. Rights in your content (exercise videos, descriptions, plans, recipes, photos, logo, brand name) stay with you.
  2. You give Ramvo a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to store, encode (for example compress video), make technically necessary copies of and show this content: to you, to your clients and, for your public profile content (photos, bio, prices, reviews), to people looking for a trainer. The licence lasts while the content is in Ramvo and ends when you delete it, except for backups until they are overwritten.
  3. Videos are private: a client watches them in the app through a short-lived link, with no download and no public link. This does not, however, stop a client from recording their screen; the client is responsible for such a breach.
  4. We will not use your content in Ramvo's advertising without your separate consent.
  5. You only add content you have the rights to (for example music in videos, photos of people who agreed). If someone reports that your content infringes their rights, we may block it while we look into it (Community Guidelines).
  6. When the agreement ends you can download your content and client data in the format the panel offers, or ask us by e-mail for an export within 30 days.

§5. Rules for working with clients

  1. You coach clients diligently and within your qualifications. You do not diagnose, treat or recommend medicines. When a client's symptoms call for it, you refer them to a doctor or physiotherapist.
  2. You do not sell, through Ramvo, prohibited substances or products that need a licence to sell, and you do not encourage their use.
  3. You tell clients clearly about prices and how you work, including session cancellations. As a business dealing with consumer clients, you are responsible for your own information and consumer-law duties.
  4. You do not write, commission or reward fake reviews, including about competitors, and you do not ask for genuine negative reviews to be removed in exchange for a benefit (Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, Schedule 20; Directive 2005/29/EC).
  5. You do not get around Ramvo's rules, for example by creating several accounts to avoid the client limit.
  6. You treat clients with respect. Harassment, sexual content and abuse of the trainer–client relationship are prohibited.

§6. Client data: controller duties

  1. Ramvo and the Trainer are independent controllers of a client's data (Article 4(7) UK GDPR and EU GDPR). Ramvo controls the data needed to run the app and the account. The Trainer controls the data they use to coach the client: what they see in the panel, what they enter themselves (for example notes, measurements, comments on injuries) and what they process outside Ramvo.
  2. As a controller, the Trainer in particular:
    • processes a client's data only to coach them and on a valid legal basis; uses the client's health data (special category, Article 9 GDPR) only to the extent the client agreed to in Ramvo, or on the basis of the client's own explicit consent given to the Trainer;
    • tells the client how they process the client's data (for example in their own privacy notice) if they also process it outside Ramvo;
    • keeps the data confidential, does not share it with third parties without a basis and does not copy health data out of Ramvo without need;
    • protects access to the account: a strong password, a locked phone and computer, no account sharing;
    • tells Ramvo at {{EMAIL}} within 48 hours about any suspected personal data breach on their account or device, so that both parties can meet the 72-hour deadline for notifying the regulator (Article 33 GDPR);
    • passes on to Ramvo any client requests about their data in the app (for example erasure) that reach the Trainer.
  3. Ramvo provides the security measures described in the Privacy Policy, including database-level access control (a client sees only their own data, a Trainer only their own clients) and encryption in transit.
  4. When the relationship with a client ends, you lose access to their current data in the app. Data you downloaded earlier outside Ramvo you keep in line with your own obligations and delete when no longer needed.
