In short

  • Only Trainers (for the panel) and AI clients (for Ramvo Premium) pay. A Trainer's clients use the app for free.
  • Trials: Trainers 30 days, AI clients 7 days. No card needed, so nothing is charged automatically.
  • A Subscription renews monthly or yearly until you cancel. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.
  • As a Consumer you have 14 days to cancel the contract. We refund you, keeping only the part for the days you already used.
  • If a payment fails, you have 7 days with full access to fix it. After that the panel becomes read-only. We never delete your data.
  • App Store and Google Play purchases are handled by the store, including refunds and cancelling.

§3. Plans and prices

  1. Current plans and prices are at ramvo.app/pricing/trainers (trainers) and ramvo.app/pricing/premium (Premium), and in the app and panel before you buy. Trainer plans differ by the limit of active clients and by features (details in the Trainer Terms, §3).

App store prices, including taxes, are set and collected by the store.

We announce a price change for an ongoing Subscription at least 30 days before the next renewal, by e-mail and in the app. If you do not accept the new price, cancel before the renewal and you will not pay the new rate.
  5. Promotional codes come with the conditions shown with the code (for example period, plan). They cannot be combined unless the code says so.

§4. Trials and free periods

  1. Trainer: a 30-day trial from sign-up. AI client: 7 days from first entering Ramvo AI mode. One trial per person.
  2. A trial needs no card and does not turn into a paid Subscription by itself. When it ends, you choose a plan or move to limited mode (§9).
  3. We remind you by e-mail: Trainers 7 days before the trial ends, AI clients 2 days before.
  4. If you buy a Subscription during a free period (trial, Founding Trainer, access granted by us) with more than 48 hours of it left, the first payment is taken only on the day the free period ends. You don't lose a day. Stripe e-mails you a reminder before the first charge.
  5. During the beta, until paid plans are switched on for your group, you use Ramvo for free. We will tell you at least 30 days before payments start.

§5. Founding Trainer and the waitlist

  1. Founding Trainer: the first 20 Trainers we accept get 24 months of free panel access, counted from the day the account is activated. Limits during that period: up to 30 active clients, up to 20 GB of their own videos, up to 100 Ramvo AI requests per client per month.
  2. We e-mail you 30 days before the free period ends. We never charge you without your explicit decision. After 24 months you can choose a plan at the prices in force on that day or move to limited mode.
  3. Not signing in to the panel for 90 days frees your Founding Trainer spot for someone else. We will e-mail you 14 days beforehand.
  4. Waitlist: anyone who joined the waitlist on ramvo.app before launch gets Ramvo Premium free forever, tied to the e-mail address used to join. It switches on automatically when you create an account with the same address. If you sign up with a different address, contact us.
  5. These benefits are personal and cannot be transferred or exchanged for money.

§6. Buying on the website (Stripe)

  1. Trainers buy a plan in the panel (Subscription tab). Payments are handled by Stripe Payments UK Ltd / Stripe Payments Europe Ltd, which processes your card details; we never see or store them.

  3. The contract is made when you confirm the payment in the Stripe window and see the confirmation. You will receive a purchase confirmation by e-mail with a summary of the terms.
  4. Renewal: the Subscription renews automatically for another period (month or year) at the same price, unless you cancel before the period ends or we change the price under §3(4). Your card is charged on the renewal date. For yearly plans we e-mail a renewal reminder at least 7 days beforehand.
  5. Invoices, payment history, changing your card or plan, and cancelling are in the Stripe customer portal, opened with "Manage subscription" in the panel.

§7. Buying in the app (App Store, Google Play)

  1. AI clients buy Ramvo Premium in the app with their Apple ID or Google Play account. The store handles payment, taxes and the receipt under its own terms (Apple Media Services Terms and Conditions, Google Play Terms of Service). We handle in-app purchases technically through RevenueCat.

  3. You manage and cancel the Subscription in your store account settings: iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions; Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  4. If the store offers a free trial, any unused part of it is forfeited when you buy a Subscription.
  5. Refunds for in-app purchases are granted by the store: Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through Google Play (on your own within 48 hours of purchase, later with our help). Your statutory rights, including the right to cancel in the UK and EU, are handled by the store as the seller. Contact us and we will help.

§8. Cancelling

  1. You can cancel at any time: on the website in the Stripe portal ("Manage subscription"), in the app store, or by e-mailing {{EMAIL}}. Cancelling is as easy as buying.
  2. After you cancel, the Subscription will not renew. You keep full access until the end of the paid period, and then the account moves to limited mode (§9).
  3. We do not refund a period that has started, except for: cancelling within 14 days (§10), a service that does not match the contract (Terms §15), a material change of the service to your disadvantage (Terms §12) and deleting an account through no fault of yours (Terms §14).

§9. Failed payments and limited mode

  1. If a payment fails, you get an e-mail and a message in the app. Stripe retries the payment automatically over a few days.
  2. For 7 days after a failed payment you keep full access (grace period). You can update your card during that time.
  3. After that the account moves to limited mode:
    • Trainer: the panel becomes read-only (no creating or editing plans, accepting new clients or AI features). Chat with clients and existing bookings keep working. The Trainer's clients keep access to their plans and logging.
    • AI client: Ramvo AI chat and new AI plans are locked. Workout, meal and progress history stays visible. You can also join a Trainer, which makes the app free.
  4. We never delete data because of non-payment. Once you pay for a plan, full access comes back straight away.

§10. Your 14-day right to cancel (Consumers)

  1. If you are a Consumer, you can cancel a Subscription contract made at a distance within 14 days of the day it was made, without giving a reason (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regs 29–30; Article 27 of the Polish Consumer Rights Act).
  2. When you buy, we ask for your express request to start the service before the 14 days are up. If you give it and then cancel within that time, we refund the full payment minus an amount proportionate to the time you used the paid plan (reg 36 of the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013; Article 35 of the Polish Consumer Rights Act). Example: a monthly plan at £20, cancelled after 6 of 30 days, refund of about £16.
  3. If no payment has been taken yet (for example you bought during a free period, §4(4)), cancelling costs you nothing.
  4. To cancel, send us a clear statement, for example by e-mail to {{EMAIL}}. You may use the model form below, but you don't have to. It is enough to send your statement before the 14 days end.
  5. We send the refund within 14 days of receiving your statement, using the same payment method unless you expressly agree to another. We charge no fee for this.
  6. For App Store or Google Play purchases, cancellation and refunds are handled by the store (§7(5)).
  7. The right to cancel does not apply to Trainers who buy a plan as a business. A Trainer who is an individual buying a plan for purposes outside their profession has it as a Consumer; in Poland it also applies to a sole trader where the contract is not of a professional nature for them (Article 38a of the Polish Consumer Rights Act).

§11. Changing plan

  1. A higher plan applies straight away. Stripe charges the price difference for the rest of the current period pro rata, at the change or on the next invoice, as the portal shows before you confirm.
  2. A lower plan applies from the next billing period. If you have more active clients than the lower plan allows, your current clients stay, but you can't accept new ones until you fit within the limit.
  3. Switching between monthly and yearly billing works like a plan change.

§12. When an AI client joins a Trainer

  1. Once the Trainer confirms you as a client, the app becomes free for you.
  2. A Premium Subscription bought on the website is automatically set to end at the end of the paid period; you pay nothing more.
  3. We cannot cancel an App Store or Google Play Subscription for you (a store restriction). The app shows you how to turn it off in the store settings.

§13. Taxes, currencies, receipts

  1. If you pay with a card in a currency other than the plan's currency, your bank sets the conversion rate and any fees.
  2. Proof of purchase (invoices, receipts) is issued by Stripe on our behalf or by the app store. We keep sales records for as long as tax law requires (6 years in the UK).
