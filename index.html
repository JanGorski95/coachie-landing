Frequently asked questions

If something's missing here, just get in touch.

Do I need a trainer to use the app?

No. Ramvo AI, the built-in virtual trainer, is available from the first minute and is enough if you never need a human trainer. You only pick a real trainer when you decide to.

How much does it cost?

For people who join the waitlist now, access to Ramvo will be free forever. Prices for everyone else are on the pricing page.

Which platforms will the app launch on, and when?

iOS and Android together, launching 1 January 2027.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Full details on what data we collect and why are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I become a trainer in the app?

Yes, we're looking for our first trainers. See the "For trainers" section above or get in touch directly.

How exactly does AI plan generation work?

You provide your goal, experience level, available equipment and any injuries, and Ramvo AI turns that into a concrete workout and meal plan you can freely edit.

Can I later switch from Ramvo AI to a real trainer?

Yes, at any time. Your history, progress and chat stay exactly as they are.

What languages is the app available in?

English and Polish.

I started building Ramvo by looking at personal trainers who create workout plans for their clients. That takes a lot of time, and just sending a finished plan is often a hassle: PDFs, messages, spreadsheets. I wanted trainers to have one app to manage their clients and plans that actually saves them time and is simply more convenient. Ramvo AI is an extra for people who don't have a trainer yet but still want to take at least minimal care of themselves. I'm building this alone, fully in the open, step by step, so if you want to follow along or have an idea for what the app is missing, just reach out.

Jan, founder of Ramvo
